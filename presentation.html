Wardrobe AI
Upload a clothing photo. Get visually similar items from a fashion catalog.
The Problem
01
Inspiration photos on Pinterest or Instagram often show seasonal or sold-out items.
02
Keyword search fails when you can only describe a visual style.
03
"Beige oversized linen blazer with subtle texture" rarely returns what you saw.
04
No easy way to go from a visual inspiration to a similar item you can buy.
Solution
1
Upload any clothing photo — Pinterest, Instagram, your own.
2
FashionCLIP encodes it into a 512-dim visual embedding.
3
FAISS searches 20,000+ DeepFashion items by cosine similarity.
4
Style, color & fit preferences rerank the results.
Scoring
score = similarity + α·goal + α·color − α·avoid
